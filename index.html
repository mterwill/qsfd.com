Quaker Springs Fire District
NOTICE TO BIDDERS

The Quaker Springs Fire District #1 (“Fire District”) in the Town of Saratoga, Saratoga County invites sealed bids for building repairs and modifications at Quaker Springs Fire District Fire Station #2 located at: 1432 NYS Rt 9P, Saratoga Springs, NY 12866. Be advised that this proposed project is a tax exempt, public works project subject to prevailing wage. PRC#2025004970. The proposal contemplates that the successful bidder will perform all work necessary to prepare the wall and roof surfaces before performing the repairs and renovations in accordance with the manufacturer’s instructions and pursuant to the specifications of the Fire District. The project includes the removal and proper disposal of all construction-related debris.

A copy of the Bid Documents can be obtained by sending an email to firedistrict@qsfd.com. An electronic copy of the Bid Document will be provided by return email. Bids must be submitted as per the Instructions to Bidders. No electronic bid submissions will be accepted.

Bids for this project will be accepted until 11:30am, Friday, June 6, 2025. Bids will be publicly opened and read at 1:30pm on June 6, 2025 at

Quaker Springs Fire Station #1, 107 Blodgett Rd, Schuylerville, NY 12871.
All additional project and bid details are contained in the Bid Documents.

Anne M Crandall

Director of Purchasing, District Administrator

Quaker Springs Fire District #1


The Quaker Springs Fire Department has been proudly protecting life and property in the Town of Saratoga since 1947. We are a 100% volunteer organization and we are always looking for additional help. If you would like to join our men and women in providing this valuable service, please contact us at (518) 584-3349 or come to a drill at Station 1 any Thursday night.
